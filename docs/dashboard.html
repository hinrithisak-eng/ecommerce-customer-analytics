If something breaks

Do not debug on stage. Say "the executed outputs are committed" and scroll the notebook, or open docs/notebook_export.html which needs no kernel at all.

Likely questions

Why synthetic data? Reproducible (seed 42) and lets me inject known errors so I can prove the pipeline catches all of them — that is what the tests assert.

Why not impute review_rating? A missing review is information (the customer chose not to review); imputing would invent opinions and bias the mean.

Why is the chi-square not significant? Sample size — 1,972 orders across 7 categories. The return-rate differences are indicative; I say so in the limitations.

Why 45 % churn risk? A fixed 180-day cutoff against a snapshot at the end of the data window; a per-customer definition is a stated next step.
